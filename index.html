Field guide

How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere. Your weapons aim and fire on their own. Esc pauses.

Hearts. You lose one when an enemy or bullet touches you, and one for every wrong test answer. The only way to get one back is five right answers in a row.

Classes set which words can show up. 学生 Student uses HSK 1, 书生 Scholar uses HSK 1–2, and 大师 Sage uses HSK 1–3. Each class also has its own starting weapons and a perk.

Level up by collecting jade drops. Each level you draft one of three characters, and each one comes with a random buff.

Pop tests arrive every 20 to 30 seconds and ask you about a character you drafted this run. A right answer raises that character's mastery (up to 3), which makes its buff stronger. It also gives a burst of XP and 6 seconds of extra damage. Five right in a row heals a heart. A wrong answer costs a heart and a mastery point.

Gold scrolls dropped by elites start a bonus test. Pass it to draft a rare character.
